Sign Talk

Breaking the silence. Bridging the gap between the Deaf community and the hearing world through advanced real-time AI.

Empowering Communication

Sign Talk is not just a translation tool; it’s a fully integrated digital bridge. Whether you are typing, speaking, or signing, our platform ensures seamless two-way communication in seconds.

Built with a state-of-the-art hybrid AI architecture, our core engine utilizes SlowFast for deep video understanding, supported by MediaPipe for precise dynamic hand cropping and ROI-guided attention. This ensures high-accuracy recognition even at 60 FPS.

Core Technologies
Python Django SlowFast MediaPipe Computer Vision WebSockets
⌨️

Text to Sign

Type your sentence and our intelligent agent instantly animates our 3D Avatar to perform the text in accurate sign language.

🎙️

Voice to Sign

Speak naturally. Our AI transcribes your voice in real-time and makes the Smart Avatar translate the audio directly into sign movements.

🎥

Sign to Text

Using just a web camera, our hybrid AI analyzes hand gestures frame-by-frame and translates them into readable text for hearing people.
